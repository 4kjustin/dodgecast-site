How it works

  1. Tell it where you're goingType an address, or tap Home or Work.
  2. See the weather on every stretchEach road is colored by the rain, snow or storms you'd meet, at the time you'd reach them.
  3. Take the dry road, or leave laterDodgecast compares leaving now, in 30 minutes or tomorrow morning, and picks the safest plan.
  4. Drive with the app you already useOne tap opens the trip in Google Maps, Waze or Apple Maps. Dodgecast keeps watching the road ahead.
