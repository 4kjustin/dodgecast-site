Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · Contact: dodgecast707@gmail.com

Dodgecast plans driving routes and departure times around the weather and warns you about storms on the road ahead. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Dodgecast.

The short version

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No servers of our own. Your trips, saved places and settings stay on your phone. We don't receive them.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling data. Dodgecast doesn't contain advertising or analytics tools, and we never sell or share your information for marketing.
  • Your location is used to plan routes and check the weather. To do that, Dodgecast sends map coordinates to the routing, address and weather services listed below.

Information the app uses

Your location

Dodgecast asks to use your location so it can:

  • plan a route from where you are, when you tap “Use my location”;
  • follow your progress during a trip you start, so it can check the weather on the road still ahead; and
  • notice if your navigation app takes a different road than planned, so it can check that road too.

“Always” location (background location) is used only while a trip you started is in progress, so Dodgecast can warn you about storms while another app (such as Google Maps, Waze or Apple Maps) is on screen. iPhone shows a blue location indicator while this is happening. When the trip ends, or you tap “End trip”, background location stops. You can allow location “While Using the App” only, or turn it off entirely, in your phone's Settings; the app still works for planning, without background alerts.

Addresses and places you enter

The addresses you type, and the places you save as Home, Work, commutes or road-trip stops, are used to plan your routes.

Information stored on your phone

Dodgecast stores the following only on your device:

  • your Home and Work addresses;
  • your recent trips (up to 8);
  • your saved commutes and their reminder times;
  • the last forecast you looked up, so it can be shown if you lose your connection;
  • your settings (theme, units, spoken alerts, demo mode); and
  • the route of a trip in progress, so background alerts can work.

This information is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time: delete saved places and commutes in Settings, remove recent trips from the main screen, or delete the app to erase everything.

Notifications

Reminders (“Time to go”, commute checks) and storm alerts are created and scheduled on your phone. We don't operate a notification server and don't receive your notification settings.

Services that receive information to provide the app's features

To calculate routes and forecasts, Dodgecast sends the minimum information each service needs, directly from your phone:

Like any internet service, these providers also see your device's IP address when the app connects to them. Their own privacy policies apply to how they handle requests. We don't control them and don't permit them to use your information for advertising.

When you tap Google Maps, Waze or Apple Maps to start navigating, Dodgecast opens that app with your start, destination and (for Google Maps) a few points along the route. From then on, that app's privacy policy applies to what you do in it.

Shared trip links

If you tap Share this trip or Share my arrival time, Dodgecast writes a message with a link to a trip page on our website. The trip (start and destination names, the route line, departure and arrival times, the weather along the way and any warnings) is packed into the part of the link after the “#”. Browsers never send that part to the website, so we don't receive or store your trip; only the people you send the link to can see it. When someone opens the link, their browser loads the map from OpenStreetMap and a fresh forecast for the places on the route from Open-Meteo.

Feedback form and launch list on our website

If you fill in the feedback form or join the launch list on our home page, your answers, and your email address if you choose to give it, are sent to us by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). We use your email only to tell you when Dodgecast launches, and you can ask us to delete your answers at any time.

Children

Dodgecast is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • Location: change or turn off location access at any time in your phone's Settings › Dodgecast › Location.
  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's Settings › Dodgecast › Notifications.
  • Saved information: remove it in the app, or delete the app to erase all of it.

Because we don't hold your data on our servers, there's nothing for us to export or delete on request. If you have questions, email us at dodgecast707@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Dodgecast handles information (for example, if we add an account system or switch service providers), we'll update this policy, change the effective date above, and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
